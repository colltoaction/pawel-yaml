Bell Labs, once the research arm of the AT&T monopoly, was a powerhouse of innovation, responsible for groundbreaking inventions like the transistor (0:29), the laser (0:54), solar cells, and the picture phone (3:08). Its success was fueled by a steady stream of funding and the brightest minds in the world. Despite the breakup of AT&T in 1984, Bell Labs continues to innovate, albeit with a narrower focus and a smaller team, still securing thousands of patents annually.
