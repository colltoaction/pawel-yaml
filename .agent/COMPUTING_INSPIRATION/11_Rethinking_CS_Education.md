# 11. Rethinking CS Education - Alan Kay

**Speaker**: Alan Kay  
**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N9c7_8Gp7gI
**Duration**: 1 hour, 10 minutes, 32 seconds  
**Channel**: Infosys Foundation USA

---

Alan Kay's keynote speech, "Rethinking CS Education," criticizes current educational approaches, particularly in computer science. He argues that education should focus on what people "need" rather than what they "want," likening ineffective teaching methods to training wheels that hinder actual learning (1:23-1:40). Kay emphasizes the importance of a long-term view in education, focusing on future generations and fostering a "richness" of understanding across various systems—natural, social, and technological (10:55-13:24). He advocates for a qualitative shift in education, moving beyond superficial learning to develop true thinking skills, which he distinguishes from mere logic (45:47-46:07).

Here is a bullet list of necessary information to reach the conclusions in the short summary:

Critique of current education: Alan Kay argues that education focuses on "wants" rather than "needs," similar to how training wheels hinder learning to ride a bike (1:23-1:40, 3:55-4:00).
Long-term educational view: Kay stresses the importance of focusing on the "next generation" (10:55-11:35) and fostering a "richness" of understanding across natural, social, and technological systems (12:38-13:24).
Qualitative shift in thinking: He differentiates true "thinking" from mere "logic," stating that current computer people often "don't even know what thinking is" (45:47-46:07).
