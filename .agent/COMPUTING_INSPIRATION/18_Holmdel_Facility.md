# 18. Holmdel 20th Anniversary - Bell Labs Facility

**URL**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BPq_ZyOvbsg
**Duration**: 13 minutes, 54 seconds  
**Channel**: AT&T Tech Channel

---

This video provides a history of the Bell Laboratories facility in Holmdel, New Jersey, celebrating its 20th anniversary. It highlights significant scientific discoveries made there, including Karl Jansky's radio astronomy breakthrough in 1933 (0:12) and the work of Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson, who won the Nobel Prize for their cosmic radiation studies in 1978 (9:13). The video also details the design and construction of the iconic Eero Saarinen-designed building (2:31) and the evolution of research and employee life at the facility from the 1930s through the early 1980s, emphasizing a culture of imagination and curiosity.

Here's a summary of key information:

Discovery of radio astronomy by Karl Jansky at Bell Labs in Holmdel, New Jersey (0:12-0:48).
Construction of the iconic Bell Labs building designed by Eero Saarinen (2:31-3:18).
Significant advancements in satellite communications including the Echo balloon test (3:28) and Telstar (4:30).
Installation of the first electronic switching system in Secaucus, New Jersey, developed at Holmdel (6:52-7:05).
Nobel Prize awarded to Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson for their work on cosmic radiation at the Crawford Hill lab (9:13-9:31).
Expansion of the Holmdel laboratory with new East and West Wings and satellite labs (9:51-10:27).
Bell Laboratories entering the 1980s at the forefront of telecommunication research (10:33-10:57).
