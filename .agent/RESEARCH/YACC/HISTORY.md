# History of YACC

YACC (Yet Another Compiler-Compiler) was developed by Stephen C. Johnson at AT&T Bell Laboratories in the early 1970s.
It was designed to complement Lex (by Mike Lesk) and create a complete language processing pipeline.

## Key Milestones
- **1975**: Original paper published.
- **Influence**: Became the standard parser generator for Unix.
- **Successors**: GNU Bison is the modern, compatible replacement used in Pawel-YAML.
